# Live Card Reader · 0.21.0

Continuously scans the Safari window (recommended) or monitor you select and automatically looks for visible playing-card faces. It displays your detected hole cards, community cards, current combination, local showdown equity and a simplified dollar EV for calling. It automatically reads the current pot, Call/Check controls, occupied players’ remaining stacks and visible card backs. The Table, My play and GTO solver tabs share Start/Stop, Show logs, Play history and an optional manual-input override. Shown opponent hands stay in memory for the current table; your own observations and measured chip intervals are saved locally. No browser integration or automated betting.

## Local GTO solver

Version 0.21 adds a native **GTO solver** workspace with heads-up postflop action mixes, sizing, EV, editable ranges/rake and measured convergence. Supported converged snapshots can drive the live suggestion; preflop/multiway remain heuristic estimates. See [GTO setup and limits](docs/GTO_SOLVER.md) and [helper source/build details](Solver/README.md). Building the app now also requires Rust; engine/dependency sources are vendored for offline builds.


## Wagers, short stacks and stable decisions

Wager OCR now retries bounded inward chip regions for each seat, including the area above your hole cards. It accepts a complete currency amount with a separately recognized S/$ chip prefix, without guessing numeric characters. Covered, conflicting and ambiguous readings stay unavailable. A blank area never becomes a fabricated zero wager.

A separately guarded **quote-priced raise response** can use the current additional Call control after two matching table/control readings, even if absolute street commitments are unreadable. It requires a known big blind, a readable consistent pot and stacks, raise evidence, a covered non-all-in price and no visible side-pot problem or contradictory known wager. Unknown commitments remain unknown. Unconfirmed controls, capped/all-in prices, short opponents, missing stacks and conflicts cannot take this path. The reason/log identifies `confirmed_call_control`; exact visible wager-gap cases retain their existing checks.

Separate **20–49 BB cash raise/fold ranges** now cover first-in early/middle/hijack/cutoff/button/small-blind play. These local heuristics are explicit in `ShortCashRange.swift` and Assumptions; they are not downloaded GTO charts. K5o is included in the small-blind opening range, 76s in the cutoff, and 95s excluded early. A standard blind-only pot, prior folds, readable legal controls and effective stacks are required. Below 20 BB remains provisional. In Balanced defense against a single standard opening raise, a confirmed dealer/raiser position and exact blind-pot reconciliation select the matching short cash range; prior calls, re-raises, extra money, multiple opponents or Tight/Loose selection prevent that substitution. Defense still uses the disclosed realization scenario and not raw showdown equity alone.

A move from independently confirmed current controls is remembered for the same cards/street. If controls, position or other strategy inputs become unreadable at the same price, its exact move/reason remains under **LAST CONFIRMED PLAN / READING UPDATE**, explicitly historical. It supplies no live EV, does not become an observed player action and does not manufacture a new Fold. A fresh changed price or incompatible readable action invalidates that retention immediately. New cards/street and Stop reset its applicability. An unpriced big-blind plan explicitly assumes no raise reaches you.

## Automatic position and cutoff opening chart

The dealer chip is recognized from its light circular border and red center beside an occupied seat. Dealer/seat geometry must agree across two readings independently of chip OCR. Position is counted clockwise through dealt seats, including folded players and excluding empty, sitting-out and new/waiting seats. The dealt roster locks for the hand; a joining/leaving player cannot silently change the position mid-hand. A changed/covered/missing dealer or ambiguous roster invalidates recognized position. A fresh hand or capture reset re-establishes it. This is tested on supplied screenshots and generated layouts; general live accuracy is not established.

The standard `$SB/$BB No Limit Hold'em` header supplies a separately confirmed big blind. A first-in classification additionally requires prior seats folded, an exact standard blind-only pot, no voluntary contribution/raise evidence and no side pots. A tracked call followed by a raise remains a raise response. Before your turn, a plan may assume earlier players fold; that is labeled conditional. Manual overrides take priority and reset with new cards. The move explanation and Hand Details show the effective position and whether it was recognized, overridden or assumed. Unrecognized position retains a disclosed hijack fallback, never a claimed cutoff read.

The cutoff membership now follows [Jonathan Little's cash-game cutoff chart](https://pokercoaching.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Small-Stakes-Poker-Cash-Games.pdf#page=31), printed page 30 (PDF page 31). The complete 13×13 grid is available under **Assumptions**, with your hand outlined. Both 76s and 98s are open-raises in the reference when folded to in the cutoff. The book uses 100 BB examples and explicitly adjusts for the players behind. The deep reference rule requires at least 50 BB effective; separate local cash rules cover 20–49 BB. Unsupported/unreadable depth remains provisional. Other position ranges remain local heuristics. None of these ranges prescribes calling a raise or guarantees profit.

## Moves-only decision headline

With a confirmed current hand, the decision headline now uses only **FOLD / CHECK / CALL / BET / RAISE**. Manual position, prior-action and big-blind entries are optional. Detailed supported recommendations retain priority. When detailed analysis cannot produce a move, a separate default policy selects a provisional plan and explains its assumptions below the move. The **Assumptions** button contains optional overrides. Raw context/recognition/calculation availability remains available for diagnostics rather than becoming the action headline.

The default uses the existing local starting-hand charts, a neutral hijack opening assumption for unknown position, observed paid/free action, tracked prior investment, made-hand features and conservative cheap-call limits. Premium starting pairs prefer a legal value raise or call; a readable free Check is preferred over folding weak hands; personal strong made hands can prefer an unsized value bet; speculative continuations require an observed additional call no greater than 20% of the final pot and 8% of the remaining hero stack. These are default heuristics, not solver solutions. An unopened-pot assumption can be wrong if earlier action was missed. The reason identifies that assumption; new observations and completed detailed calculations replace the plan.

Fresh coherent readable action controls restrict the displayed move. In standard Hold’em, a paid Call also permits Fold even when OCR misses the Fold label. With no verified controls, the display is explicitly **CONDITIONAL** on the client offering that move on your turn. A default is marked **ESTIMATE** when current actions are readable but other strategy inputs remain missing. No exact bet/raise size, pot, range equity or dollar EV is manufactured. Side-pot eligibility and unreliable amounts still suppress the corresponding financial calculations even though a provisional move remains visible. A move in this state is a hypothesis with lower confidence, not a guaranteed legal current action or profitable recommendation.

The last move or plan actually displayed for the same hand stays unchanged after folding or a missing-card gap under **LAST MOVE / PLAN · SAVED**. A new confirmed hand clears it; Stop/capture reset clears all presentation history. Suggested plans never become observed player actions or opponent statistics. Before any cards are confirmed, and for an already folded/all-in hand with no earlier recorded move, the headline uses an em dash: no new legal betting decision exists in those states. A partial board gets a cautious conditional plan and is never evaluated as a fresh preflop hand. The default and strict analysis are logged separately, with provisional/historical flags; existing private log retention and no-image policies are unchanged.


## Responding to a preflop raise after calling

Automatic mode now has a separate **RAISE RESPONSE** branch. A tracked own call/raise, tracked or visible opponent raise, user-confirmed Facing a raise, a wager above the configured big blind, or a wager greater than twice your committed chips under the standard-blind assumption can establish raised-pot context. An old first-in selection does not override this evidence. Prior action is taken from confirmed labels or the existing conservative chip/pot tracker, never from the app’s suggested action. Unknown position uses a disclosed conservative scenario rather than blocking a supported raise response. A standard small-blind completion is not a raise. A positive additional price from the big blind, or a price above the standard blind option, can establish a raised-price scenario when the blind is confirmed.

With consistent visible wagers or the guarded confirmed-Call-price scenario, legal Call/Fold controls, a readable pot/stacks and supported single-pot eligibility, the app compares the **additional** call price with equity against assumed ranges. Earlier calls are sunk; they remain part of the current pot and are not charged again. It suggests CALL or FOLD with the current price, range equity, required equity and assumptions. AA/KK’s separate premium-value Raise rule remains available. This response compares calling with folding; it does not solve the EV of every re-raise or bluff. Missing prices, conflicting bets, shown opponent cards, unresolved side pots and short multiway contribution eligibility still withhold unsupported advice and name the missing input.

Choose **Position / ranges → Raiser assumption**: Tight (`TT+,AQs+,AKo`), Balanced (`77+,ATs+,KJs+,QJs,JTs,AJo+,KQo`) or Loose (the full list is shown in the panel). A tracked own raise or at least two tracked opponent raises selects the corresponding tighter re-raise scenario. One aggressor range plus a disclosed broad caller range for each other active player is an assumption, not inferred player identities or learned ranges. Opponent statistics are not used as proof of a range. Missed actions can leave raise depth unknown.

The response runs **100,000 separate local range-conditioned deals**, including blockers, multiple opponents and fractional ties. Each legal combo in each named range has equal weight. Independent combo tuples are drawn and the whole tuple is rejected on any collision; filtering later ranges sequentially would bias earlier players’ weights. The 95% individual sampling bound is about ±0.43 percentage points. A bounded cache reuses the same cards/ranges when only the current pot or call changes. Jobs are cancellable and old-hand results cannot restore advice. These deals are separate from the one-million uniform-random deals in the headline, not added to them or to historical hands.

For non-all-in decisions, this implementation uses explicitly **uncalibrated realization scenarios**: 90% of range equity for cutoff/button, 75% for other confirmed positions, 80% when position is unknown. These are conservative scenario parameters, not measured probabilities or solver results. If hero cannot bet again or every opponent is already all-in, supported eligibility uses full range equity. CALL is chosen when scenario-adjusted equity covers the price; FOLD otherwise. Sampling uncertainty is separate from the much larger uncertainty about ranges, realization, future play and rake. Legal re-raise opportunities and implied odds may change the best strategy. No zero-error or profitability claim is made.

Headline showdown equity and call EV remain explicitly against random hands. The raise explanation and conditional Range equity metric use the response model; saved actual-decision price metrics use its raw range equity. Mathematical Max call excludes the realization discount and is not a strategic maximum investment. Full explanations are available by hovering the fixed reason area and through Position / ranges. HandTraces record range preset, explicit range lists, tracked-action evidence, response equity/sample count and the displayed explanation. General diagnostics remain metadata-only.

Qualitative strategy context: [PokerStars: entering a cash-game pot](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/lesson/entering-a-cash-game-pot/) and [3-betting preflop](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/lesson/3-betting-preflop/). These support considering price, range, position and remaining action; the local ranges and realization parameters above are implementation assumptions, not imported charts or validated solver policies.


## Clear recognition and decision status

The panel distinguishes **Confirming hole cards**, **Reading community cards**, **Cards unreadable**, **Reading table details**, and missing strategy context. A current confirmed hand no longer receives a generic missing-hand explanation just because prices, opponents or action controls are unresolved. General diagnostics record the displayed availability state separately from the underlying live-action state, plus a confirmed-hole count. Local hand traces include the recognition stage and current independently confirmed hole cards.

Two hole cards can remain confirmed and visible while an incomplete board prevents a complete current hand. This keeps the hole cards current without treating a partial flop as preflop or using an old board for a new live decision. Shrinking board readings with the same hole cards suppress live math until the street is readable again; fully repeated board readings can correct a previous accepted card. Cards disappear from the current-hole channel immediately when missing/partial/ambiguous; old display cards stay muted and historical. A different confirmed hole pair with an incomplete board cannot inherit the previous hand’s suggestion. No recognition acceptance thresholds were lowered. Identical hole cards with no clear new-round cue remain ambiguous and may require Stop → Start.

If **Cards unreadable** remains while cards are visible in Safari, that is a recognition/source issue. Select the Safari poker window, keep the table tab open, and supply a fresh screenshot plus general diagnostic log if it persists. A status change does not establish that unreadable card artwork was fixed.


## Before-turn plans, postflop value and local hand traces

The **BEFORE YOUR TURN** panel starts a conditional plan after two consistent card/player observations, before Call/Check controls appear. It assumes players between a reliably detected current actor and you fold. Readable all-ins remain; if turn order is unknown, everyone remains. A precise confirmed preflop position can establish fallback order on a complete roster. Actual calls, raises, folds, stacks and prices update the projection. Projected folds never enter player statistics. Position still needs manual confirmation; an unsupported preflop situation shows its missing context instead of inventing an opening decision.

The headline **Showdown equity** always counts actually active opponents against uniform random legal hands. **Plan equity** and **Forecast call EV** use the explicitly projected opponents. They can differ. Equity calculations no longer wait for readable Call/Check controls, prices or stacks; dollar EV still requires supported monetary readings. A mathematical completion never counts as an additional table observation. Actual advice uses independently confirmed real action controls. After folding, preserve the last actual suggestion; if none existed, preserve the last conditional plan under **LAST FORECAST**.

Free checking is no longer an unconditional shortcut for strong made hands. A transparent value policy considers whether your own cards make the hand, board pairing, possible flushes/straights, readable stacks, side pots and available actions. For example, 9–6 on an unpaired 9/6/A rainbow flop can suggest **BET** when checking is free. A narrow limped-pot cutoff/button rule can suggest **RAISE** against one modest first flop wager. It does not raise automatically with board-only two pair, dangerous textures, large bets or unresolved side pots. Missing strong-hand value inputs show their reason instead of silently calling Check best. Half-pot language is only a starting guide; legal sizing is chosen in the poker client. These are limited heuristics, not a solver or a guarantee of correct play. Qualitative context: [PokerStars multiway pots](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/strategies/a-guide-to-multiway-pots/) and [PokerCoaching multiway pots](https://pokercoaching.com/blog/mastering-multiway-pots/).

At your request, a separate local **HandTraces** log now contains confirmed own hole/board cards, anonymous current seat chips/activity/actions, observed controls, confirmation flags, position/pot context, actual and displayed recommendation, reason, conditional assumptions, model/sample count and available EV. It records changed states and asynchronous calculation results, with observation and event timestamps. Open it from **Show logs → Hand decision traces (cards & chips)**. Folder: `~/Library/Application Support/LiveCardReader/HandTraces`. No screenshots, raw OCR, website URLs, credentials, opponent shown-card history or persistent opponent identities are written. Files are owner-only, rotated at about 2 MB into three files per launch; on launch only five previous files are retained (up to about 16 MB plus the largest record). Delete the folder to remove traces. Capture-end events separate sessions. Recognition may miss actions and identical consecutive starting cards can share a trace identifier; these are observed traces, not authoritative complete hand histories. General diagnostics remain metadata-only. No past missing board can be recovered retroactively from old metadata-only logs.

## Full-window dashboard

The app opens maximized to the current display’s usable desktop area, keeping native window controls, menu bar and Dock available. Use the header’s expand button to fill the screen again after resizing. The layout adapts across the entire window from a 1000×720-point content area upward and has no main scroll container.

A full-width probability/EV summary sits above three aligned columns. Showdown Outcome and expanded Hand Details share the left column, with Hand Details directly below the outcome chart. The center contains a plain-language suggested action, calling metrics and final-hand probabilities. Player stacks and session-only action rates / shown cards share tabs in the right column; your cards and the community cards remain in a bottom dock. Panel heights, row spacing and selected text/card sizes grow with the window, keeping labels stable during live reading updates.

The action panel separates **CUTOFF CASH CHART**, **FIRST-IN RULE**, **PREFLOP RULE** and **PRICE MODEL**. Click **Assumptions** in that panel to confirm your position, whether everyone folded / someone limped / someone raised, and the big blind. Position and prior action reset for each new detected hand; the big blind stays until capture resets. Dealer/seat recognition and standard-blind-only first-in detection supply automatic context when confirmed; overrides remain optional. Full missed preflop history is not reconstructed from a single frame. Your effective position and its source appear in Hand Details.

The deep first-in cash-game reference covers early/middle/hijack/cutoff/button at ≥50 BB; separate local ranges cover 20–49 BB including the small blind. Both require a standard unopened blind pot and supported readable inputs. In this supported context, **98s in the cutoff suggests RAISE**, choosing a legal size on the client. It is outside the conservative early-position opening range. These are limited transparent heuristics, not solved opening ranges or a claim that the quoted raise slider is a good size. General limped-pot, ante/straddle and unreadable situations require more strategy context; supported blind-defense and raised prices use the range-response model. These situations do not receive a strategic Fold merely because a random-hand check-down call estimate is negative. Position principles and suited connectors are discussed in [PokerStars Learn's starting-hand lesson](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/lesson/poker-starting-hands/). Position affects this preflop policy and the narrow postflop value-raise rule; general position-dependent postflop strategy remains unimplemented.

For supported postflop prices, the price model shows CALL / FOLD based on incremental check-down call EV. A sampling interval spanning zero retains a low-confidence point-estimate choice and explanation. Future betting, actual ranges, fold responses and rake remain unmodeled.

Before the flop, **AA / KK** follow a separate premium-pair cash-game rule: prefer **RAISE** with independently confirmed current Raise controls, sufficient readable hero chips, consistent current amounts and another opponent who can potentially continue. Show a dollar raise-to amount only when consecutive complete table readings and conservative sizing/covering-stack checks support it. Otherwise suggest the action without an invented amount and explicitly direct the user to the table’s legal sizing controls. If no supported raise is available, the paid-call fallback is **CALL** under the premium starting-hand rule, rather than folding it solely because a many-way random-hand check-down model is negative. This does not infer a tight re-raising range or optimal sizing; the qualitative assumption follows [PokerStars Learn’s discussion of preflop pocket kings](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/strategies/how-to-play-pocket-kings-when-an-ace-hits-the-flop/). The existing narrowly verified heads-up unbeatable-river raise remains available.

For hands outside the eligible value-bet rule, **CHECK** can appear as soon as two current observations confirm an active hero, a free Check button and no contradictory visible wager. It does not wait for a simulation, the pot, opponent-stack OCR or a moving raise slider, because checking adds no chips. Eligible strong value hands first need the value-bet inputs. Its reason makes clear that this does not establish whether betting would earn more. Control confirmation resets on new cards, a changed call price, missing controls or hero changes; an old action is never carried into the next hand. Paid price-model EV still needs its supported pot/stack/eligibility inputs. Known opponent faces continue to suppress priced advice because their cards are not included in the random-hand engine.

The dollar headline **Check-down call EV** assumes every counted opponent reaches showdown with no later bets or rake. That is a different scenario from a value raise, so its sign can disagree with a separate preflop rule. After folding, the last supported suggested action, reason and basis stay unchanged under **LAST SUGGESTION**, including while what-if board calculations continue. The saved action is presentation-only; it never becomes a live decision, and clears for a different detected hand or capture reset. History retains only a move or plan actually displayed for that hand. Input readiness is explained below the move and in diagnostics; defaults fill the move display when detailed advice is unavailable. Saved suggestions are explicitly historical; absent current action buttons never imply a fresh call, fold or raise. Reasons have a fixed three-line area and a full-text tooltip; ordinary values continue updating inline.

Action OCR normalizes whitespace in merged/multiline labels (for example, `CALL\n$0.10` and `CALL$0.10`) without guessing monetary digits. If the whole-table pass has no supported Call/Check price, a focused pass retries the bounded action-button area on the same current frame. Ambiguous, malformed and low-confidence prices are still rejected. Interrupted probability calculations automatically retry on subsequent current observations, with bounded backoff, without requiring new cards. Metadata-only diagnostics include advice availability, a generic state code and independent control-confirmation status; reasons, cards and monetary amounts are not logged.

Inline metrics include **pot odds** (eligible pot / new call), **equity edge** (equity minus required call equity in percentage points), **max call** (equity × eligible pot / (1 − equity), capped at a readable hero stack and rounded down), and **effective SPR** (largest effective remaining-stack matchup / eligible pot). Multiway effective matchups differ; unwagered stacks are not part of the pot. Maximum call holds the current pot and equity fixed; it is a hypothetical price, not a prediction of a changed opponent bet/range. The information button retains minimum-pot explanations and the explicit-assumption heads-up raise scenario calculator.

Hand Details now shows the street, starting class, current made hand, a best-five-card selection, board texture, category-improvement probabilities, improving-card list, exact runout count and historical sample context. Equivalent best-five selections may exist in tied hands. Historical samples and category improvements do not guarantee winning. The **?** button and Hand Details **…** popover retain extended definitions and lists. Select the Safari poker window and keep its table tab open. For monitor sharing, keep the table unobstructed.

## Opponent and personal tracking

The **Players** tab displays Play / Raise / Fold / Check rates, their sample counts and the latest confirmed shown cards for each occupied opponent seat. Play history contains full counts, PFR samples and all recorded reveals. The header’s chart icon opens **Play history** for all recorded reveals and your own archive. Scanning and table tracking continue after your hole cards disappear, including a preflop fold, as long as the anchored table remains readable. Two consistent recognized faces at one seat are required; partial, duplicated, conflicting or unassigned faces are rejected. Repeated frames and later streets of the same observed round do not create duplicate samples. River reveals are labeled as such rather than asserted to prove showdown. Very short reveals can be missed at the capture/recognition cadence.

Opponent profiles use uninterrupted seat geometry, readable seat badges when available, and NEW PLAYER markers. Sorted screen-position IDs are not persistent player IDs. A seat missing from two valid observations is forgotten without resetting the other seats. A new-player marker or changed readable badge starts a new profile; confirmed new seats start empty. Stop, capture interruption, a >2.5 second visibility gap, or a changed hero seat/layout clears the whole table session. **End table & stop scanning** and **Clear this seat** provide explicit resets. **An anonymous replacement or switch to a table with identical visible geometry can be impossible to distinguish. End the table before switching.** Round counts are observed samples, not complete dealt-hand counts or unbiased showdown rates. Profiles never affect the uniform-random live equity model.

Your archive saves only your cards, visible board, observation timestamps, observed chip/pot amounts, saved decision estimates and own table balance checkpoints. Opponent profiles are not Codable and never enter the archive or exports. The file is `~/Library/Application Support/LiveCardReader/History/own-play.json`, written atomically with owner-only file permissions. It persists across launches; unfinished hands/intervals are closed as unmeasured on restart. An unreadable existing file is preserved and new tracking remains memory-only, with a visible error. **Export my history** writes a hero-only JSON copy at your chosen location.

**My statistics** is a separate main-page tab for all saved personal play, with observed net, gross measured winnings/losses, wins/losses/even results, measured/unmeasured counts, a cumulative result chart, action-mix bars, Play and preflop raise rates, raise/fold/check/call/bet counts, largest win/loss, maximum drawdown, measured win rate and average result. Known raise additions show the amount coverage rather than inventing unreadable sizes. Hand history includes confirmed own actions and observed wager-to amounts. Statistics remain available while scanning continues.

Action tracking reads high-confidence Fold / Check / Call / Bet / Raise labels in expanded, focused seat regions outside the available action controls. A label needs two consecutive readings, or one label corroborated by matching stack reduction and current-bet increase. Persistent labels count once. A separate conservative tracker can **infer** an action when two stable before/after states show exactly one player's stack decrease, a matching pot increase, a readable current wager, and enough prior betting information to distinguish call/bet/raise. Checks require a unique confirmed turn moving away with unchanged money and no outstanding call; folds require that turn movement plus disappeared cards while at least two players remain. Initial blinds, stack changes alone, no-change frames, card disappearance alone, multiple simultaneous chip changes, incomplete money, side pots and capture gaps do not produce inferred actions.

The Players panel shows **n=0** for occupied seats without action samples; it never substitutes a false measured 0%. A **\*** marks rates that include inferred actions. Rows also show observed round, action and visible-flop counts even when no action labels have been read. History lists recent actions with their label/chips/turn/cards provenance and separate label/estimate counts. Flop visibility includes partial observed rounds; it is not VPIP. Inferred actions are estimates, not a complete hand history. Opponent timer artwork and short live labels still need real-table validation. Available hero buttons establish a hero turn, not a completed hero action.

Seat/reveal/explicit-label tracking can continue through missing pot OCR on an already confirmed layout, while automatic dollar EV remains unavailable. A fresh layout requires a unique readable pot. Resized or moved cards can reacquire a distant anchor only after current table/pot/hero geometry corroborates the new pair, followed by normal two-frame card confirmation.

**Play %** = preflop hands with a recorded call/bet/raise ÷ preflop hands with any recorded action. **Preflop raise %** = sampled preflop hands with a recorded raise ÷ the same sampled hands. Forced blinds alone do not count. **Raise / Fold / Check / Call / Bet %** = that action’s events (including marked estimates) ÷ all recorded events across streets; a hand may contain multiple actions. Counts and denominators are visible. These are sampled rates and can differ from complete-history [VPIP/PFR definitions](https://www.pokertracker.com/guides/PT3/general/statistical-reference-guide). Missing actions can bias results, not just reduce sample size. Hands saved by v0.11.0 load unchanged with no action entries; new action statistics start from current recognized or marked inferred events. A card disappearance alone remains a possible fold/muck/reading-gap observation, not a fold count.

Money results use stable **preflop stack + visible current bet** checkpoints on consecutive observed deals. A first hand already on the flop/turn/river has no opening bankroll checkpoint. The next confirmed deal can close the preceding chip interval; leaving mid-hand cannot invent a final payout. Missing deals or identical preflop cards without a clear boundary may combine an interval, and unreadable amounts prevent supported measurement. These are screen-derived estimates, not account statements. Top-ups and cash removals cannot reliably be distinguished from poker results: record **Added to table** / **Removed from table** in personal history after the initial checkpoint. Adjustments apply at the next checkpoint; pending cash changes are shown separately. Record them before the next measured deal. Overall net totals measured table intervals only, and excludes unfinished final intervals. No exact account profit, complete action log or bb/100 is claimed.

## Start



For a fresh checkout, run `bash scripts/build-app.sh` first. The generated app is in `dist/Live Card Reader.app`; build outputs are excluded from Git. This project requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 15.2+ Xcode and Rust installed.

1. Open `dist/Live Card Reader.app` on this Apple silicon Mac (macOS 15.2+).
2. Click **Start**, then choose the **Safari poker window** (recommended), or the monitor containing it in Apple's screen-sharing picker. Complete any macOS screen-recording permission prompt.
3. Keep one table open in the selected Safari window. Window sharing avoids capturing the dashboard over the table. With monitor sharing, keep the cards uncovered and put this app beside the table or on another monitor. Minimized/closed windows, sleep, a different Safari tab or unreadable artwork can still interrupt recognition.
4. Leave it running. **Stop** ends capture and clears the hand.

Start once per sharing session. Folding, the board clearing and a new deal do not require Stop/Start. After two matching observations, the reader remembers the card positions and checks those areas on every subsequent frame, alongside its full-screen search. It retains those positions between hands and through stale/failed readings, but reads card values anew. After your cards disappear, saved-card review can continue probabilities as the anchored board changes. Your last confirmed cards are labeled hypothetical and the last decision EV stays frozen. Missing or uncertain board readings pause updates while scanning continues. Previous confirmed values remain muted with an Updating label; they clear on a confirmed changed hand, Stop or capture loss. Explicit Stop, a new source-selection session, sleep or macOS ending screen sharing clears the remembered layout; capture requires a new Start after sharing ends.

The selected source is streamed continuously, requesting up to five frames per second. Recognition keeps only the newest pending frame; actual scan rate depends on local OCR work and hardware. This version uses only the stream authorized through Apple's sharing picker; it makes no separate screenshot requests. An explicit stream idle event confirms unchanged content and allows the latest frame to be scanned again. Blank, suspended, stopped, invalid or unreadable frames discard the cached image; silence does not renew old readings. Safari tab titles and window resizing no longer stop reading, and no manual layout calibration is required. A user-initiated activity keeps background processing active while allowing normal system sleep. Sleep or capture revocation stops reading; click Start to resume afterward.

The footer shows received frames, completed scans, attempted candidates and recognized faces. The status distinguishes a received screen with no card-shaped areas, an excluded screen area, and ranks/suits that do not match. The detector supports conventional light faces and large centered suits. The supplied screenshot regressions cover Q-clubs/5-clubs, A-clubs/J-hearts with a flop, and 9-diamonds/5-diamonds with a flop, while ignoring miniature previews and history faces. ## Automatic table inputs, equity and dollar EV

**Read table automatically** is on by default. Keep one entire table visible, including every occupied seat’s stack/card backs, the pot and your Call or Check button. The reader uses local Vision OCR and relative table geometry, alongside card recognition. It displays all detected remaining stacks and each active opponent’s effective remaining stack, `min(your remaining stack, their remaining stack)`. Seat numbers in the dashboard identify screen locations, not Bovada player identities.

The number of opponents comes from visible card backs. Sitting-out and new-player labels are excluded; seats with no visible cards are assumed out of the current hand. No hidden cards or betting ranges are inferred. The two supplied table screenshots pass with all occupied stacks, pot and zero-cost Check detected; generated six- and nine-seat layouts also test a separate Call amount, current street bets and side-pot labels. Other actual layouts and rendering states still need validation.

Money is parsed strictly into integer cents, with no guessed OCR character substitutions. Cards and relevant table data must agree across consecutive observations. Changed or missing required amounts immediately pause current EV. Previously confirmed values remain in a separate presentation snapshot, muted and age-marked; they never feed a current calculation. When the same confirmed cards/opponent count return, a bounded 16-entry in-memory math cache reuses that probability result to prevent repeated Monte Carlo fluctuations. A confirmed new hole-card hand, Stop, stale capture or capture interruption clears the presentation. During saved-card review, matching board extensions retain last-decision EV and update what-if probabilities. Manual mode changes clear incompatible EV.

Every label, chart row and seat row has a fixed place. The dashboard reserves a You row and eight opponent-position rows, with placeholders for unused positions. Currency and percentages update inline with monospaced digits and no insertion/removal animations. Card tiles keep a dark background in empty, live and updating states. Missing Call/Check controls show a waiting status; probabilities can remain current while call EV waits for a decision. Inactive players’ remaining stacks are excluded from EV confirmation requirements, so their OCR jitter does not interrupt a supported estimate. Large chip labels located inward from known seats are treated as bets instead of automatically triggering a missing-stack warning. Ambiguous active-player or pot eligibility readings still withhold current EV.

Once cards and active opponent count are confirmed, the app calculates your expected share of a shared pot at showdown, including split pots. Each opponent is dealt a uniformly random legal hand. Preflop and multiway postflop use 1,000,000 local Monte Carlo deals; heads-up postflop enumerates all legal outcomes (1,070,190 flop, 45,540 turn, 990 river). Calculation runs separately from screen recognition.

Dollar EV uses the **current pot before your call**, including existing bets and prior contributions, and the **additional amount you must call**. Your new call is excluded from the pot input. `EV = equity × (eligible pot + call) − call` measures incremental value relative to folding; prior contributions are sunk. A Check button supplies a zero additional call and models checking through to showdown. The break-even calling equity is `call / (eligible pot + call)`.

Stacks cap the new call at your remaining chips. For a heads-up all-in, both players’ current street bets must be readable to resolve an uncapped amount-to-call; any unmatched opponent wager is removed from the eligible pot. Opponents’ remaining stacks show future betting exposure; they do not change showdown hand strength or add unwagered money to the current pot. Existing side pots, multiway short all-ins, unreadable active stacks or ambiguous activity/controls pause current EV with a specific waiting/eligibility message. Previous values remain visibly muted while data updates. Resolving arbitrary side pots requires complete contribution history, which this screen-only reader does not reconstruct.

This remains a **showdown model**: everyone reaching showdown, one pot with equal eligibility after the supported adjustment, no later bets, no future folds and no rake. Opponent ranges, future actions, position-dependent strategy and recognition errors are not modeled. It is not a solver or a complete prediction of action profitability.

To use explicit assumptions instead, switch **Read table automatically** off. Manual mode provides an opponent-count stepper (1–8) and dollar pot/call fields. Its single-pot formula does not use detected stacks or side-pot adjustments. Manual inputs reset when cards/opponent count change, readings become stale or capture stops; update them yourself as betting changes. The app never silently switches to manual values when automatic reading fails.

Monte Carlo results show a conservative two-sided 95% sampling bound using Hoeffding’s inequality, `sqrt(log(40) / (2 × samples))`; EV’s range propagates that bound through the same eligible pot and call. This covers simulation noise under the random-hand model, separately from unquantified range/recognition uncertainty. Exact river enumeration has no simulation noise. Old jobs are cancelled and ignored after recognized cards/opponents change.

Formula references: [expected value](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/lesson/examining-tournament-ev/), [pot odds](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/lesson/pot-odds/), and [Hoeffding’s original paper](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830).

## Probability charts and definitions

The two fixed charts use a 0–100% scale. **Win outright** is the chance of beating every opponent. **Tie for best** is the chance of sharing first place; a tie beaten by another player counts as **Lose**. These three mutually exclusive outcomes sum to 100% before rounding. **Pot share** is showdown equity, counting only your fraction of a split pot, and therefore differs from tie probability. **Call threshold** is the required equity for zero call EV, a cost threshold rather than an outcome forecast.

The second chart displays the probability of finishing with each best-five-card category: high card, one pair, two pair, three of a kind, straight, flush, full house, four of a kind and straight flush (including royal flush). Exactly one category applies per deal, and the board can supply some or all of the best five cards. These probabilities sum to 100% before rounding and do not by themselves predict winning. Outcomes and categories are collected from the same simulation/enumeration as equity, including multiway splits.

The **?** help popover explains EV, pot/call/eligible pot, remaining/effective stacks, opponent assumptions and simulation uncertainty. Hover over a bar or amount for its individual definition. Sampled bars share the existing conservative 95% bound **for each metric individually**; this is not a simultaneous guarantee across every bar. Exact river enumeration has no simulation noise. Muted charts and amounts are visibly marked as previous confirmed readings while current data updates.

## What identification means

The detector proposes card rectangles using Vision and bright connected regions, reads rank/suit glyph shapes, and groups nearby cards into rows. Joined white faces can be separated using their top caps; the full width below the rank is searched for a large suit. Condensed rank templates and small alignment/width tolerances handle font and resize differences without lowering the acceptance thresholds. Miniature faces are filtered relative to the largest recognized faces before grouping and duplicate checks.

It assumes the single isolated two-card row below a possible three-to-five-card board is your hand. It does not know your account or seat identity, or semantically identify the live table versus a historical image. With multiple comparable candidate pairs, comparable duplicate faces, ambiguous row layouts or uncertain glyph matches it shows no hand rather than selecting one. Keep one live table visible and avoid other large card images.

Cards must agree across successive scans before being treated as current. Changed, missing or ambiguous readings pause current calculations except for explicitly labeled saved-card review after your hole cards disappear. Previous confirmed data can remain muted in its fixed cells while scanning continues; a confirmed changed hand or capture invalidation clears that presentation. The combination label uses only recognized cards; unread board cards can change the complete hand's category. Rank and suit similarity scores are geometric match scores, not calibrated probabilities of correctness.

The app ignores only the visible parts of its own windows and a still-open previous reader version, including their recently moved positions. Reader windows fully covered by Safari or located on another macOS Space no longer mask the playing surface. This fixes a possible cause of perpetual 0 faces. Its window still physically covers content behind it, so keep it away from the table's cards.

## Local processing

No accounts, cloud service, API keys, browser extension or network requests. Frames stay in memory: the latest stream image is cached for explicit idle events, alongside the processing frame and latest waiting frame. The cache is replaced on a new complete frame and discarded on interruption or stop; images are never saved to disk. Diagnostic metadata and the hero-only personal history described above are saved separately. Opponent cards/profiles remain in process memory and are cleared on session exit. Old versions’ other history/settings files remain untouched.
